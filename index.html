ABOUT

Syntax, theory, research.

Parisyntax is a permanent seminar dedicated to syntax. It provides a regular space for presenting ongoing research, discussing new ideas, and exchanging perspectives on syntactic theory and empirical research.

Ideally, every other seminar will be given by a student.
